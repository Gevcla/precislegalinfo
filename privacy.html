précis Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

précis ("we," "us," "our") is developed by Gevan Claridy. This policy explains what information précis collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. précis helps you build and polish résumés using AI, and this policy covers the mobile app and the services it talks to.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and authentication data, handled by our authentication provider, Clerk.
  • Résumé content you provide: your name, work history, education, skills, career summary, and target job details — either typed directly into the app or extracted from a résumé PDF you choose to upload.
  • Subscription information: your subscription status and related product/transaction identifiers, provided to us by Apple's In-App Purchase system via RevenueCat. We never receive or store your payment card details — Apple handles your payment directly.
  • Basic usage data: request counts we use to enforce reasonable rate limits and prevent abuse of our AI features. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not collect advertising identifiers or behavioral tracking data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To generate and refine résumé content using Anthropic's Claude AI models. When you generate or regenerate a résumé, the relevant résumé details are sent to Anthropic for processing. If you upload a PDF résumé, its contents are sent directly to Anthropic to extract structured data — the uploaded file itself is never stored on our servers and is discarded immediately after processing.
  • To process subscriptions and manage your entitlements through Apple and RevenueCat.
  • To enforce rate limits and keep the service reliable and free of abuse.

How Your Information Is Stored

Your account's résumé data is stored using Supabase, our database provider, protected by row-level security policies that restrict access to your own data. Data is encrypted in transit. Your information may be processed or stored in countries other than the one you live in.

Who We Share Data With

We share data only with the service providers that help us run précis:

  • Clerk — authentication and account management.
  • Supabase — database hosting for your résumé data.
  • Anthropic — AI processing to generate and parse résumé content.
  • Apple / RevenueCat — subscription and payment processing.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your résumés are retained until you delete them. You can delete an individual résumé at any time from your dashboard, or permanently delete your entire account — including all saved résumés — from Dashboard → Delete account. You can also request deletion or ask about your data by emailing us at precisresumeapp@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

précis is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email precisresumeapp@gmail.com.

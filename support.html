Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Download the app, create an account, and follow the on-screen setup. The whole process takes less than a minute.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Tap "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen and enter your email. We'll send you a reset code — enter it along with a new password to regain access.

How do I contact support?

Email us at precisresumeapp@gmail.com. We typically respond within one business day.
